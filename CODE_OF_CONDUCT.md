# Code of conduct

WClop is a small project, and we want it to be a welcoming one for everyone who uses it or contributes to it, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source;
- assuming good intent, and asking before concluding;
- giving and taking constructive criticism of ideas and code, not of people;
- crediting other people's work.

Things that aren't acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind;
- discriminatory jokes or language;
- publishing anyone's private information without their permission;
- any other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project) and to everyone taking part, maintainers included.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ogshrug), or by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/ogshrug/WClop/security/advisories/new) if you'd rather keep it inside the project. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Decisions are explained to the person concerned where it's safe to do so.
